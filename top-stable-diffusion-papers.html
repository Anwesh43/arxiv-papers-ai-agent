Stable Diffusion Segmentation for Biomedical Images with Single-step Reverse Process

Authors: Tianyu Lin, Zhiguang Chen, Zhonghao Yan, Weijiang Yu, Fudan Zheng
Published: 2024-06-26
arXiv: 2406.18361v3

Diffusion models are effective across generative tasks, but medical segmentation faces compute/time issues and typically needs multi-step reverse processes. SDSeg is a latent diffusion segmentation model built on Stable Diffusion, using latent estimation for a single-step reverse process and latent fusion concatenation to avoid multiple samples. It reports strong results across five benchmarks and provides code.
